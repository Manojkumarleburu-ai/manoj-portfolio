ForecastIQ: demand forecasting for food waste

Academic project

An AI-powered forecasting solution for quick service restaurants and cloud kitchens. It combines historical sales, wastage, weather, holiday and promotion data to forecast item-level demand, then converts the forecast into recommended preparation quantities to reduce waste and stock-outs.
